# Incident Response Plan

Required for Mission-critical. Record owner, date, candidate/spec revision and human review.

## Owner and escalation

Name the incident owner, backup contact and escalation path.

## Detection and containment

List alert sources, severity criteria and authorized containment actions.

## Recovery and communication

Describe restore/rollback, integrity checks, affected-user communication and follow-up.

## Exercise results

Record a rehearsal scenario, date, evidence, gaps and owner of fixes.
