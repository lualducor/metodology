## Outcome

Record ship/kill/pause, date, user outcome and supporting observations.

## Estimate vs actual

Compare estimated/actual total hours and explain the material differences.

## What the process caught

Link gate IDs and concrete defects/near-misses, including the relevant exposure count.

## What it missed

Describe escaped defects, intended earlier detection and why it failed. Note rubber-stamped checks.

## The one rule to change

Propose one simplification or improvement, with cost, evidence and consequences; do not invent a finding.

## Process overhead

Record hours spent on process and percentage of total effort.

## Review time and rework

Record review minutes, oldest pending item and rework hours.

## Rule exposure and consequences

For each candidate rule: ID, times applicable, catches/misses, cost, consequence of failure and alternative protection. No catches alone is not evidence to remove a rare severe-risk control.
