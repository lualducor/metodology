# Risk Radar

Copy to `docs/risks.md`. Replace prompts with evidence; use `n/a: reason` only when justified.

## Risk

Describe cause, event and consequence; identify the exposed data/users.

## Severity and impact

Explain likelihood, blast radius and reversibility; do not classify by effort alone.

## Mitigation

Record preventive/detective controls and how their effectiveness is checked.

## Acceptance and expiry

Record accepting owner, rationale, expiry and condition that reopens the risk.
