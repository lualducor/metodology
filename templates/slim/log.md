# Slim log

Replace each instruction with project-specific evidence. See the worked example.

## Decisions

Record meaningful choices and rationale. Presence is required; there is no entry quota.

## Risks and failures

Record impact, mitigation, owner, acceptance expiry, catches/misses and follow-up. Keep routine test output in test logs.

## Tasks and AI review

Record task, AC, scope, verification, review estimate, actual review time, queue age, AI session/run reference and verdict on exact revision.

## Retro

Record outcome, estimate/actual, process overhead, review time/rework, catches/misses and one rule proposal with exposure, cost and consequences.
