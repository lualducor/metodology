# Privacy Review

Required for Mission-critical. Record owner, date, candidate/spec revision and human review.

## Data inventory

List data categories, purpose, sensitivity, source and destinations without copying personal records.

## Collection and retention

Explain minimization, consent/authority where applicable, retention and disposal.

## Access and deletion

Document access boundaries, export/deletion paths and verified behavior.

## Review findings

Record reviewer, evidence, unresolved risks and follow-up. Do not claim legal compliance from a checklist.
